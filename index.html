Behind the Sky

Rattan crawlers rise from the vines and grow back unless burned. The Hornbill lends you its sight. Every gift is a loan, paid at the crossroads.

  • Crawlers you cut down grow back after 4 seconds. Stand by the pile and tap Burn it, or shoot a fire arrow. Light fire at the brazier.
  • Survive 5 waves to make the clearing safe.
  • Sight slows time and shows hidden crawlers, but adds debt. At 100 the dark closes in.
  • Pay at the crossroads with rice or iron. Forge better iron at the forge to Sii's drum.

Phone: left thumb moves, drag the right side to look around.
Computer: mouse looks · left click cut · hold right click to aim the bow · WASD or arrow keys move · Space roll · F sight · Q or wheel switch arrows · E burn/pay/forge · Esc pause
